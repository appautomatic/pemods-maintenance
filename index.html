What a displacement puck does

It fills the dead air above your glans. The less empty volume in the cylinder, the less air the pump has to move, so you reach your target vacuum faster.

You feel it most with Milking and RIP, with a manual pump or a slower electric one, and in larger cylinders with a lot of dead space. Karl's original concept reported 50 to 60% shorter transition times.

More photos, details and discussion in the PEMods post on Reddit.

Magnetic, no O-rings

Embedded magnets hold the stack together. A central air channel keeps air flowing, nothing seals.

Fully modular

No special top piece. The magnets only let the pucks stack the right way round.

Easy to remove

Two magnets on top of the cylinder hold the stack. Take them off and it drops right out. No rod, no hook.

Easy to clean

Nothing traps fluid or wears out. Pull apart, rinse, done.

Where do you ship?

From Switzerland to Switzerland, Europe, Canada and Australia. Other countries on request by email.

Will I pay customs?

Outside Switzerland, your local customs may charge import VAT and a small handling fee on delivery. These are paid by the recipient and are not part of our price.

How many pucks do I need?

Fill most of the empty space above your glans and leave about 1 cm at the top. Include at least one 10 mm puck so you can adjust in small steps. The measuring tool above does the math.

Can I return custom sizes?

No, custom pucks are made to your measurement and can't be returned.
